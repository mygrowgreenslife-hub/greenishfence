Welcome to Greenishfence

Your own private corner
of Wayanad's green hills

Behind a fence of green, there's a quiet home with a private pool, a garden and room for everyone you love.

Mornings are slow coffee on the sit-out, afternoons are spent in the water, and evenings end around the campfire with a barbecue and a home-cooked meal.

Greenishfence is booked by one group at a time, so the pool, the garden and the common hall are only for you.

Plan your stay

Beyond the fence

Explore Wayanad

Wildlife safaris, dams, misty peaks and ancient caves. Some of Wayanad's best sights are right around Sultan Bathery. Ask us for routes and timings.

Close to Greenishfence

  1. 01

    Karapuzha Dam

    A scenic earthen dam with landscaped gardens and wide reservoir views.

  2. 02

    Muthanga Wildlife Sanctuary

    Jeep safaris through forest that is home to elephants, deer and more.

  3. 03

    Kolagappara Hills

    A short hill trek to sweeping views over the Wayanad valleys.

  4. 04

    Cheengeri Rock

    An adventure hike up a rocky hilltop, often above a sea of morning mist.

  5. 05

    Edakkal Caves

    Neolithic rock carvings on the slopes of Ambukuthi Mala.

Around Wayanad

  1. 06

    Chembra Peak

    A trek to the famous heart-shaped lake near the summit.

  2. 07

    Banasura Sagar Dam

    India's largest earthen dam, with speedboats and island views.

  3. 08

    Soochipara Falls

    A three-tiered waterfall deep in the forest.

  4. 09

    Pookode Lake

    A freshwater lake for pedal boating, ringed by evergreen forest.

  5. 10

    Kuruva Island

    River islets on the Kabini, best explored by bamboo raft.
